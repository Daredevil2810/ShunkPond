Seahorse races

Bet

Fair race. All six seahorses are identical. Each one gets random speed bursts from your browser's secure random number generator, and the first across the line wins, so every seahorse has exactly a 1 in 6 chance. A win pays 6x your bet, so on average you break even. Shunk is in-game money only.
