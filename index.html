Where and when

How to fish

Cast, wait for the bite, then hit Hook. Hold the button (or Space, or press the picture) to lift the green zone and keep the fish inside it until the gold bar fills.

Out of bait? You get 2 free worms every day, and the shop sells better bait.

Bait shop

You get 2 free worms every new day. Better bait means better odds of rare, heavy fish. Your bait box has a limit, so spend or upgrade it. "Fill box" buys as many as fit in the box and as you can afford, and the price on the button is what it will cost.

Workshop

Upgrade your gear. Heavier fish need a stronger line, or the fight gets harder.

Pearl dive

Fortune machine

Choose a fish below, then pull the lever or press SPIN. The reels land on a random multiplier from x0.50 to x1.50, and every value is equally likely, so on average you break even. Your fish's weight and price change by that multiplier. Only the fish you choose is at stake.

Decorations

Decorations add appeal, which earns more tips from visitors. Toys (duck, ball, crab, hoop, music box, orb) are alive: tap them, drag them, flick them. Your fish play with them too.

Your bag

Put up to 5 fish in each tank. You can take them back out any time.

Seahorse races

Bet

Fair race. All six seahorses are identical. Each one gets random speed bursts from your browser's secure random number generator, and the first across the line wins, so every seahorse has exactly a 1 in 6 chance. A win pays 6x your bet, so on average you break even. Shunk is in-game money only.
